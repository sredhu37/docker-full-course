A web app that helps us to practice der/die/das in German with Flashcards.

Front of the card: a noun in German without the article (e.g. "Tisch") with the English translation in parentheses (e.g. "table"), and an image. Users will see 3 options for the article (der/die/das) and will have to select the correct one before flipping the card to see the answer. A small button to pass the card without answering will also be available.
User can click on the card to flip it and see the answer on the back of the card: the correct article (der/die/das) and the noun in German (e.g. "der Tisch"). If der, use blue background color, if die, use pink background color, and if das, use gray background color.

The app will have a database of common German nouns and their corresponding articles, along with image URLs to help with memorization. If URL is not available, a default image will be shown.
The app will be responsive and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices, allowing users to practice their German articles anytime and anywhere.
The quiz will provide immediate feedback and track the user's performance over time.
